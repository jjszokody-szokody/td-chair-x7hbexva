PRODUCER’S CHAIR

You're the producer of a live sports telecast. The director and TD run the switcher. You run the show: the clock, the breaks, the sponsors, the replays, the stories, and the talent in your ear. Hit every break window, deliver every billboard, and land on the hard out to the second.

⏱ Time is compressed. Game coverage runs about 4× real time, the body of a break 16×, and long stoppages 8×. The TIME chip shows the current rate. Each show is about 5–8 minutes in real time.

Want to sit in the TD chair instead? ▶ Play TD Chair (the switcher game)

How to produce

Your job: keep the show on time and on content. The director and TD do the button pushing. You decide what airs and when.

THE LOOP

1) Top of show: select SHOW OPEN, press S to give the director a heads-up, and G at 0:00:00. 2) Opening billboard during the first stoppage. 3) Big play? EVS offers angles. Pick the best 1–2 in order (1–4), R to roll, and get out before the next snap. 4) Feed stats (ZXC) at stoppages. 5) Breaks: when the window opens, wait for a stoppage, then T 30 to break, W wrap, set up and GO the break. Near the end, I counts the talent back in (3–12 s left). 6) Sponsor elements (sponsored stat, promo) must air inside their windows. 7) Final whistle: watch O/U, use D to lengthen, shorten, or drop post-game pieces, then billboard, close, and W wrap about 10 s before the hard out.

NEVER

Never talk in IFB during a live play, a play-by-play call, or the last 5 s of the countdown. Never leave a replay or a full-screen element up when the ball is snapped.

PRODUCER TERMS

Hard out: the exact second the network takes the feed back. Go over and you get clipped, go under and you leave dead air.
Back-time: counting backward from the hard out to find when each remaining piece must start (the B/T column).
Over / under: projected end minus hard out. +0:20 means you're 20 s heavy, so cut something.
MC (Master Control): the network. It owns break windows, the hard out, stretches and cuts.
Break window / media timeout: when the network expects a commercial break. Miss it and MC has to shuffle inventory.
Billboard: “brought to you by…” graphic plus talent read. Contracted, so it must air.
Make-good: free airtime owed to a sponsor when their element misses its window.
IFB: interruptible foldback, the producer's voice in the talent's earpiece.
30 to break / Wrap: warnings in the talent's ear. Skip them and you upcut them mid-sentence.
Toss: talent hands off to another reporter (“let's go down to the sideline”).
Count in: “back in 5, 4, 3…” so talent is ready when the break ends.
Heads-up / set up: telling the director what's next so the TD can preset it.
EVS / replay ops: replay servers. ISO = a camera on one player. Super-mo = high-frame-rate slow motion. Reverse = the opposite side.
Pocket: a natural stoppage long enough for a feature or a sponsor element.
Stretch / cut short: MC moves your hard out later or earlier.
PL: the private line, the crew's intercom (the TRUCK COMMS feed).

🎙 Director Booth

Record your own calls and the game plays them back as the director. Whole calls play as is when you've recorded them. Anything else gets stitched from short segments ("ready", "take", "camera", the numbers…). A missing piece falls back to the synthesized voice for just that word. Silence is trimmed automatically. Talk at show volume, about a hand's width from the mic.

Click ENABLE MIC to start. The mic only works on the https link or on localhost.
