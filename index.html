TD CHAIR

You're the technical director on a live sports show, working an original Kayenne-style panel. It's inspired by the Grass Valley Kayenne K-Frame layout but not affiliated with or endorsed by Grass Valley. The director calls the show in control-room language. A ready call is your warning to set preset, transition type and keys. Execute on the action call. Reaction time is scored from the moment the action call lands. Hot punches, jumped calls, wrong sources and keys left up all go on the mistakes list.

Want the other chair? ▶ Play Producer’s Chair: run the show, the clock, breaks, sponsors and replays.

HOW CALLS MAP TO THE PANEL

"Ready 2… take 2": 2 on preset, CUT on "take". "Ready to dissolve to 4… dissolve": 4 on preset with MIX, then AUTO or the T-bar. "Ready to wipe to graphics… wipe": GFX on preset with WIPE, then AUTO.

"Ready replay A… roll and take": RPL A on preset. The EVS op rolls on the call and you take it. "Ready the open… roll it, take VT" works the same way.

"Key it" / "Lose it" toggles Key 1, and "Bug in" / "Lose the bug" toggles Key 2. You can also delegate a key in Next Transition and take it with the background.

"Go to break, fade to black": BLACK on preset with MIX, and the bug has to go. Pros delegate BKGD + KEY 2 and do it in one AUTO, and E-MEM 3 sets that up. E-MEM 2 loads the M/E 2 two-box to preset.

Preset-bus moves are free because they're off-air. Changing program without a call, executing before the action call, or leaving the lower third keyed into a replay, graphic or black costs points.

🎙 Director Booth

Record your own calls and the game plays them back as the director. Whole calls play as is when you've recorded them. Anything else gets stitched from short segments ("ready", "take", "camera", the numbers…). A missing piece falls back to the synthesized voice for just that word. Silence is trimmed automatically. Talk at show volume, about a hand's width from the mic.

Tap ENABLE MIC to start. The mic only works on the https link or on localhost.
